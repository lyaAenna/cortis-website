WHAT IS CORTIS

BY: STAY

Welcome to the ultimate hub for everything CORTIS.

Meaning

CORTIS stands for "Color Outside the Lines," representing a 5-member South Korean boy group (Martin, James, Juhoon, Seonghyeon, and Keonho)[cite: 6].

It symbolizes thinking freely, breaking away from conventional standards, and self-produced creativity in music and video[cite: 6].

Martin (Leader)

Background: Canadian-Korean.

Key Traits: Stands out for his extreme height (around 190 cm / 6'3").

Role Focus: Primarily operates as the group's leader, main rapper, and vocalist.

James

Background: Thai-Chinese / Taiwanese heritage, born in Hong Kong; he is the oldest member of the group (born 2005).

Key Traits: A former member of the pre-debut group Trainee A who is highly multilingual, speaking five languages.

Role Focus: Heavily involved as a main dancer, rapper, and vocalist.

Juhoon

Background: South Korean.

Key Traits: Worked as a child model before pursuing an idol career.

Role Focus: Frequently highlighted as the group's visual alongside his work as a vocalist and rapper.

Keonho

Background: South Korean.

Key Traits: The youngest member (maknae) of the group, born in February 2009, and a former competitive swimmer.

Role Focus: Contributes as a versatile vocalist and dancer.

Seonghyeon

Background: South Korean.

Key Traits: Had one of the longest preparation periods in the group, training for 5 years before debuting.

Role Focus: Delivers strong musicality as a main vocalist and dancer.
